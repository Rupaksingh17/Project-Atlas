## Executive Overview
The global residential water purifier market is shaped by expanding urbanization, heightened consumer awareness regarding tap water contaminants, and the proliferation of multi-stage purification technologies.

<!-- Page 2 -->
## Product Technologies
Purification methods analyzed include Reverse Osmosis (RO), Ultraviolet (UV) disinfection, Ultrafiltration (UF), and gravity-based filtration systems.

<!-- Page 3 -->
## Market Sizing and 2025-2030 Outlook
The global residential water purifier market was valued at USD 9.15 billion in 2024. The sector is forecast to expand at a CAGR of 8.4% from 2025 to 2030, reaching USD 14.88 billion by 2030. Point-of-use under-the-sink and countertop units dominate consumer demand.
