## Scope and Application
Commercial water treatment encompasses municipal water recycling, commercial facility boiler feedwater treatment, and commercial building greywater filtration systems.

<!-- Page 2 -->
## Market Sizing and Forecast
The commercial water treatment systems sector was sized at USD 5.30 billion in 2024, projected to expand at 7.1% annually through 2029. Membrane filtration systems and chemical softening account for majority revenues.
