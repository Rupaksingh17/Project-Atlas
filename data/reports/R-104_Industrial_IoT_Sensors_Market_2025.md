## Market Definition
Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT) sensors provide continuous condition monitoring across factory machinery, predictive maintenance telemetry, and automated environmental monitoring.

## Market Sizing and Growth Projections
The global IIoT sensors market stood at USD 14.60 billion in 2024. Expanding at a CAGR of 13.5% through 2030, growth is catalyzed by Industry 4.0 digitization initiatives in automotive and heavy equipment manufacturing.
